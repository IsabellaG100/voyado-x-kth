# Team 6 Mission — Analytics Overview

Build an executive-facing analytics dashboard that gives leadership a clear, visual snapshot of business health. The dashboard surfaces revenue trends, campaign performance, and channel breakdowns — all switchable across time ranges for different perspectives.

- **Lead with KPIs**: Display four hero metrics (revenue, active customers, average order value, retention rate) with trend indicators and color-coded changes so executives can assess performance in seconds.
- **Visualize trends and performance**: Render a 12-month revenue bar chart and a campaign performance table with inline visual bars, making patterns and top performers immediately scannable.
- **Slice by time range**: Offer a date range selector (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 12 months) that updates all dashboard data instantly, enabling quick comparisons across different time horizons.
