# Team 4 Mission — Campaign Builder

Give marketers a guided, wizard-style workflow for creating and managing multi-channel marketing campaigns. From channel selection to content editing with live merge-tag previews, the builder makes campaign creation fast, visual, and error-free.

- **Guide the creation flow**: Walk marketers through a 4-step wizard — choose channel (email/SMS/push), pick a template, edit content with merge tags, and review & schedule — with clear step indicators and back/next navigation.
- **Preview personalized content**: Render merge tags like `{{first_name}}` and `{{tier}}` with sample data in a live preview pane, so marketers see exactly what recipients will receive before sending.
- **Manage campaign lifecycle**: Display all campaigns with status badges (draft, scheduled, sent, paused) and provide status management controls with valid transition rules to keep the workflow organized.
