# Team 2 Mission — Product Catalog

Deliver a polished e-commerce browsing experience where loyalty members can explore products, discover what they love, and curate a personal wishlist. The catalog combines filtering, search, and sorting to make finding the right product fast and enjoyable.

- **Browse with ease**: Present products in a responsive grid with category filters, real-time search, and multiple sort options so shoppers can quickly narrow down thousands of items.
- **Engage through details**: Offer rich product detail views with descriptions, ratings, stock status, and tags — giving members the information they need to make confident choices.
- **Personalize the experience**: Let members heart their favorite products into a wishlist, creating a personal collection that persists across filters and search queries.
