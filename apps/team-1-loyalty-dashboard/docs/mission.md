# Team 1 Mission — Loyalty Dashboard

Build a clear, scannable loyalty program dashboard that empowers program managers to monitor health and performance at a glance. The dashboard surfaces key metrics, tier breakdowns, enrollment trends, and member activity — giving stakeholders the quick insights they need without deep analytics.

- **Track what matters**: Display KPIs (total members, active members, points balance, points issued) with trend indicators so managers instantly see if the program is growing or declining.
- **Visualize the membership**: Show tier distribution and enrollment trends through intuitive CSS-based charts, making seasonal patterns and tier balance easy to spot.
- **Surface real-time activity**: Present a live feed of recent loyalty events and a top-members leaderboard to highlight engagement and identify the most valuable members.
